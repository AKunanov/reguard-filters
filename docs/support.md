# reguard Support

## Turning reguard on

1. Open the reguard app.
2. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions (iOS 18 and later; on iOS 15–17, Settings → Safari → Extensions). On iOS 26.2 and later, the button in the app opens it directly. On a Mac, open Safari → Settings → Extensions.
3. Turn on **reguard**, **reguard Ads** and **reguard Annoyances**.
4. Allow **reguard** on all websites.

The app shows which content blockers are on and when the filter lists were last updated.

## A site doesn't work properly

Tap the reguard icon in the Safari toolbar and switch off **Protection on this site**. The page reloads without reguard and without the content blockers. Switch it back on the same way.

## An ad or redirect still gets through

Send the page address to the email below. Ads that the filter lists miss are reported to the EasyList authors.
