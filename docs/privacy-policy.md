# reguard Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

reguard does not collect, store or share personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

- **The reguard Safari extension** watches taps on web pages to stop navigations you didn't ask for. It runs on your device and does not send page contents, addresses or your browsing history anywhere.
- **The reguard Ads and reguard Annoyances content blockers** give Safari a list of rules. Safari applies them itself; reguard never sees which pages you visit or what gets blocked.
- **Sites you switch protection off for** are saved on your device so the extension and the content blockers can skip them.

## What reguard downloads

The app downloads the content blocker rules from GitHub (github.com and release-assets.githubusercontent.com) when you first open it and then up to twice a day. These are plain downloads: reguard sends no identifiers or personal data with them. As with any internet request, GitHub receives your IP address; see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children

reguard collects no data from anyone, including children.
